🇵🇹 The Portuguese Reading List 🇵🇹



Beginners

Os Gaiatos

Set in the lively streets of Lisbon, these short stories follow a group of local kids through a day full of mischief, friendship, and city adventures.

Quer Coxinha?

Follow the characters through situations involving Brazilian food, friendships, travel, and daily routines.

O Pequeno Príncipe

When a pilot crashes in the Sahara Desert, he meets a mysterious young prince from a tiny asteroid who begins to share his wise, poetic reflections.

Continuers

A Bolsa Amarela

Raquel is a young girl struggling to fit in, so she hides her three biggest wishes: to grow up, to have been born a boy, and to become a writer, all inside a bright yellow bag.

O Alquimista

A young Andalusian shepherd travels from Spain to the Egyptian desert in search of a hidden treasure, learning about destiny, courage, and following one's dreams along the way.

Tudo por um Pop Star

Three teenage best friends from a small town hatch a wild plan to travel to Rio de Janeiro to see their favorite boy band live in concert.
